## Questions the AI should ask

Every task could be analyzed using questions like:

### Goal

* What do you want?
* Why do you want it?
* What happens if you don't do it?

### Timing

* Why today?
* Is today the best time?
* Can it be postponed?
* Deadline?

### Cost

* Online cost
* Offline cost
* Delivery cost
* Waiting cost
* Opportunity cost (what else could you do instead?)

### Time

* How long will it take?
* Can it be finished today?
* Why will it take that long?
* Can AI reduce the time?

### Resources

* What tools are required?
* What skills are required?
* Any prerequisites?
* Any permissions or approvals?

### Alternatives

* Is there another way?
* Which option is cheaper?
* Which option is faster?
* Which option gives the best quality?

### AI Analysis

* Is this worth doing?
* Priority (1–10)
* Difficulty
* Cost-benefit analysis
* Risks
* Suggested plan
* write a business plan with low budget starting this bussiness.
